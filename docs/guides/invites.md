# Inviting Teammates

Invite teammates to your LEADR account so they can help manage games and boards. All team members share access to all games and boards on the account.

## Prerequisites

- [Download and install the LEADR app](./install.md)
- A LEADR account:
  - [Register](./register.md)
  - [Join a team](./join.md)

## Send an Invite

![Sending an invite](../assets/images/invite.gif)

### Step 1: Open the Invite Screen

From the LEADR app dashboard, press `i` to open the **Invite** screen.

### Step 2: Enter Invitee Details

You'll be prompted to enter:

1. **Email address** - The email address of the person you're inviting
2. **Display name** - Their display name (optional, defaults to email prefix)

### Step 3: Confirm and Send

Review the invitation details. Press `Y` or `Enter` to send the invite, or `N` or `Esc` to go back and make changes.

### Step 4: Done!

Press `Enter` or `Esc` to return to the dashboard

## What Happens Next

Your invitee will receive an email with:

- A 6-digit verification code
- Instructions for joining your team

They'll use this code to complete the join process in the LEADR app. See [Join a Team](./join.md) for the full process from their perspective.

## Invite Permissions

All team members have equal access to:

- All games on the account
- All boards on the account
- Creating new games and boards
- Editing existing games and boards
- Viewing and resolving score flags

!!! info "No role-based permissions (yet)"

    Currently, LEADR doesn't have role-based access control. All team members have full access. If this is a feature you'd like to see, let us know on [Discord](https://discord.gg/RMUukcAxSZ)!
