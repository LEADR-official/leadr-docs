# LEADR Documentation

**Cross-platform leaderboards for game developers**. Add feature-rich leaderboards to your game on any platform, any engine, and unify all your players
 in one place. Open-source, with built-in anti-cheat and more, without trying to be your entire backend.

## Get Started

Create your account and first leaderboard in minutes. No credit card required.

[Quick Start](quick-start.md){ .md-button .md-button--primary }
